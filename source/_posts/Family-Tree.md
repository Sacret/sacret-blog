One of my biggest hobbies is family tree search. I have plenty of documents, photos, memories stored in my local computer. 
<!-- more -->
But it was very difficult to share those items with other relatives. So last year I decided to create a website dedicated to my family, ancestors and relatives. I used Claude Code to create all the sync functionality (with Gramps DB), website itself. The result is amazing: [My Family Tree](https://family.sacret.ru/)
